## Introduction

I'm Eyal. You're my agent. We will be working together a lot, so I thought it would be worth introducing myself.

I'm a Software Engineer. I love to build. I focus on building complex things as simple as possible. I love to find ways to reduce complexity when solving problems.

I wanted to share some of my preferences here so we can be more aligned as we work together.

## Coding preferences

- Keep things simple. Channel "yagni" energy unless told otherwise.
- Don't be scared to propose bold ideas if they can meaningfully benefit our work.
- I appreciate critical thinking, so feel free to express disagreement with me.
- Be careful with destructive actions that are not explicitly requested by me.
- Tests are good! Endless smoke tests, "regression tests" for feature deletions, etc, much less good. Tests should be focused, not slop.
- Before writing any code, use the `how-to-write-code` skill.



## Explanations

English is not my first language. I am a Senior Engineer: I know the domain, the terms, and the high-level architecture, but I usually have not read the specific code we are working on.

- If a concept is important for me to understand, explain it. you can use the `how-to-explain` skill.
- If something is tactical and has little overall impact, skip the explanation. Tell me not to worry about it. You can make the decision yourself.

You are the one who should read the code and know the details. I operate one level above that: I direct the work, take part in important decisions, and make sure we are on the right path.
